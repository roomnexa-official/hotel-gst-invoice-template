# Hotel GST Invoice Templates (Free)

Free, GST-ready invoice templates built for Indian hotels — room folios, restaurant bills, and B2B invoices with GSTIN. Use them in Excel or print to PDF. No signup, no cost.

## What's inside

| Template | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `room-folio-gst.xlsx` | Room charges with GST slab applied on declared tariff |
| `restaurant-bill-gst.xlsx` | Food & beverage billing, split from room folio |
| `b2b-invoice-gstin.xlsx` | Corporate guests — invoice with their GSTIN |

## Before you use these

- Verify GST slab rates against your room's declared tariff (slabs change — check current CBIC guidance or ask your CA).
- These templates handle the math; they don't replace your accountant's review.
