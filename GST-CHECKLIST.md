# GST Checklist for Indian Hotels

- Room tariff GST slabs change from time to time — always verify current slabs against CBIC guidance or ask your CA before billing.
- B2B invoices must carry the guest company's GSTIN.
- Keep restaurant (F&B) billing split from the room folio.
- E-invoicing: check whether current turnover thresholds apply to your property.
- These templates handle the math — get your CA's sign-off before using them for real billing.
